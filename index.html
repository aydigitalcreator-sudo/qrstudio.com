What is a QR code used for?

A QR code stores a link or piece of text as a pattern that any phone camera can scan. Businesses use them on menus, packaging, and posters to send people straight to a website without typing a URL. Event organizers put them on tickets, and people add them to business cards so contact details can be saved with one scan.

How to scan a QR code

Open your phone's camera app and point it at the code — most phones show a link banner automatically, no separate scanner app needed. If nothing appears, check that your camera app supports QR scanning in its settings.
